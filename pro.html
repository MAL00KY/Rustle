What happens after you pay

You get a licence key by email, immediately. Paste it into Rustle once and that is the last you think about it. It works on every computer you own - your desktop, your laptop, and the one you have not bought yet. Nothing counts them, because there is no server to count with.

If the internet is down

Rustle keeps working. The key is checked when you first enter it and then only occasionally, and a check that fails never stops you dictating. An app that dies because a server did not answer is not one you should trust with your work.
