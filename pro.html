What Pro adds

It writes it properly

Ramble, stop, start again, say "um", and you still get clean prose. A small language model on your own machine turns what you said into what you meant, and none of it is sent anywhere.

Per-app voices

Full sentences and proper punctuation in your email, something shorter and more relaxed in a chat window, and no automatic capitals in a code editor. You set the voice once for each program.

In your language too

The free version hears English. Pro listens in the other languages the speech engine was trained on, 99 in all, and follows your keyboard, so when you switch to Arabic with Win+Space, Rustle listens in Arabic. Rambling Arabic comes out as clean Arabic. How well it hears a language depends on the language: it is at its best in the widely spoken ones. If it does not hear yours well enough, the fourteen-day refund covers it.
