What Pro adds

It writes it properly

Ramble, stop, start again, say "um", and you still get clean prose. A small language model on your own machine turns what you said into what you meant, and none of it is sent anywhere.

Per-app voices

Full sentences and proper punctuation in your email, something shorter and more relaxed in a chat window, and no automatic capitals in a code editor. You set the voice once for each program.

The free version hears English. Pro hears all ninety-nine languages the engine knows and follows your keyboard, so when you switch to Arabic with Win+Space, Rustle listens in Arabic. Rambling Arabic comes out as clean Arabic.

If you download it directly, Windows will warn you

The simplest way around this is to get Rustle from the Microsoft Store. The Store version is signed by Microsoft, so there is no warning, and you can buy Pro from inside it. Everything below only applies if you download Rustle from this website.

Rustle is not signed with a certificate, so Windows shows a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC". It does that for every program from a developer it has not seen before, whatever the program does.

Click More info, then Run anyway.

The boring, important part

What happens after you pay

You get a licence key by email. Paste it into Rustle once and you can forget about it. The key works on every computer you own, including ones you have not bought yet. Nothing counts your computers, because there is no server to count them.

The key is the same whether you installed Rustle from the Microsoft Store or from here, so you are not tied to whichever one you picked first.

If the internet is down

Rustle keeps working. The key is checked on your own computer rather than online, so Pro works without an internet connection.

Refunds

Updates

Your words

Pro is exactly as private as the free version. Your speech is turned into text on your own machine, and none of it leaves. The writing model is downloaded once, from the same places as the speech engine, and then runs offline. The privacy policy has the details.
