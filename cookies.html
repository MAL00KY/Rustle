Cookie policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

This website sets no cookies of its own.

It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it keeps nothing in your browser. That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to ask about.

What loads when you visit

The pages, their stylesheet and their two typefaces all come from this site. The typefaces are kept here on purpose rather than fetched from Google, so that reading this site does not tell any other company you were here.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records the IP address of each visit for security, as any web host does, and sets no cookies on these pages. The GitHub Privacy Statement covers what it does with that.

When you press Buy

Rustle Pro is sold through Paddle.com. Pressing a Buy button on the Rustle Pro page loads Paddle's checkout from Paddle's own servers. Until you press it, nothing of Paddle's is on the page.

Once the checkout has loaded, Paddle, and the checkout analytics it runs alongside it (called Retain, served from profitwell.com), may store cookies or similar data in your browser. Paddle uses them to run the checkout, to prevent fraud and to measure how the checkout performs. They are Paddle's, not mine, and are described in Paddle's privacy notice, under cookies. You can clear them at any time in your browser's settings.

Links to other sites

The Microsoft Store, GitHub and the other sites this one links to have their own cookie policies, which apply once you follow a link there.

The Rustle app

The Rustle app for Windows does not use cookies. What it keeps on your computer is listed in the privacy policy.

If this changes

If this site ever adds anything that stores something in your browser before you ask for it, this page changes first, and the site asks you before it happens. Questions can go to malix.kayat@gmail.com.
