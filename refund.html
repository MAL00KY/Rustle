You can get your money back for Rustle Pro within 14 days of buying it, for any reason.

What happens next

You get the full amount back, to the card or account you paid with. Paddle processes the refund, and how long it takes to show up depends on your bank. Once it has gone through, please stop using the licence key from that order.

After 14 days

Refunds after 14 days are up to me. If something is wrong with Rustle Pro, write to me anyway and I will try to put it right.

Your legal rights

If you live somewhere that gives you a legal right to cancel online purchases, such as the EU or the UK, this policy does not reduce that right.
